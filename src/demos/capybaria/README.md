
# Capybaria 

## Brainstorming
- A very little game, about capybaras
- Four-legged capybaras, equipped with a variety of weapons, must fight in controlled environments for their supremacy.
- 3rd person game, multiplayer.
- Capybaras can move, jump and run. 
- Weapons are attached to both sides of capybaras, using a belt.
- Weapons include:
    - Machineguns 
    - Rocket launcher
    - Granade launcher
    - Rail guns
- Capybaras also have defensive spells
    - damage reduction
    - damage amplification
    - Reflect attack
    - Self-heal
